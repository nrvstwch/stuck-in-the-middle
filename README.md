# Stuck in the Middle

A silly browser wave-survival game. Your feet are glued to the floor, you can't move, and everything wants to bonk you.

**▶ Play it here:** https://nrvstwch.github.io/stuck-in-the-middle/

## How it plays
- You stand in the middle; your weapon swings by itself at anything inside the dashed circle.
- Enemies spawn just out of reach and close in from every direction. Waves keep coming.
- Every 10th wave a boss shows up.
- Spend gold in the shop any time: 8 increasingly ridiculous weapons (Wet Noodle → Duck Gatling), 7 armors (Bathrobe → Disco Plate Mail) and 7 stats.
- Defeated foes sometimes drop snacks that fly to you and heal you.
- Died? Revive for gold (the price climbs each time) and use the shop first to buff up.
- The goal: buy out the whole store, then see how long you last. Most runs end somewhere past wave 100.

## Controls
- **P / Esc**: pause
- **+ / -**: game speed (1× to 10×)
- **N**: skip the break between waves

## Running it locally
It's a single file with no dependencies: open `index.html` in any modern browser.
